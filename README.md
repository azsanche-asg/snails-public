# Snails

AI services for small tasks you’d rather hand over.

[Snails at Burrito’s Labs](https://snails.burritoslabs.com/)

## Technology

A web service with structured task intake, AI production and checking, and scheduled digital delivery.

## Architecture

The service turns a chosen task into a reviewed brief with a scope, price and delivery target. AI creates, checks and refines the result within the task’s scope.

## Product behavior

Customers agree to the offer before payment. The service website explains its available tasks, examples and AI processing policy.
